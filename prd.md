# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Load Africa

## Product Type

Logistics Marketplace Platform

## Goal

Connect customers who need transportation services with verified transporters and drivers across Africa.

---

## User Roles

1. Customer
2. Driver / Transporter
3. Broker
4. Admin

---

## Core Features

### Customer

* Instant Quote
* Create Booking
* Vehicle Selection
* Real Time Tracking
* Booking History
* Profile Management

### Driver

* Registration
* Vehicle Management
* Load Acceptance
* Trip Management
* Earnings Dashboard

### Broker

* Lead Management
* Booking Management
* Commission Tracking

### Admin

* User Management
* Driver Approval
* Vehicle Management
* Booking Management
* Payment Reports
