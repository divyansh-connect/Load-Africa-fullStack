# Client User Guide

Welcome to LoadAfrica! This guide explains how to use our platform to book cargo transportation and rent plant machinery.

---

## 1. How to Log In
1. Open the website.
2. Click **Login** on the top right.
3. Enter your registered email address and password.
4. Click **Sign In**. You will be directed to your personalized dashboard.

---

## 2. How to Book Cargo Transport
1. Navigate to the **Book Transport** page from the sidebar menu.
2. Enter pickup and delivery addresses.
3. Choose the appropriate vehicle category (e.g., Flatbed, Tri-Axle).
4. Enter cargo description (cargo name, weight in Tons, volume, and special instructions).
5. Click **Review Booking** to preview your route on the map.
6. Click **Confirm & Submit**. The request will be sent to the broker network.

---

## 3. How Plant Hire Works
1. Navigate to the **Book Plant Machine** page.
2. Choose from the available machinery list (e.g., Excavator, Loader, Flatbed).
3. Select whether you require an operator with the machine.
4. Specify dates and click **Book Machine**.
5. The machine supplier will review your request and confirm availability.
